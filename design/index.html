BillerPe Owner App

Design v1 · 5 Oct 2026. A phone app for owners on the Web POS + Captain App plan. The owner sees every outlet from anywhere, downloads reports, gets alerts, and manages menu, staff, tables and settings. The app never takes orders. Every screen scrolls, and links between screens work (tap a card, a tab or the back arrow).

Sample data only Android first Online only · data from the BillerPe cloud Outlet data is up to ~3 min old
1 · Sign in & watch 2 · Reports & alerts 3 · Manage

1 · Sign in & watch every outlet

Home adds up all outlets. Each outlet shows whether its PC is online, what is running right now, and every bill with who did what. Everything here is view only.

2 · Reports & alerts

Every Web POS report, for any date range, for one outlet or all outlets together, downloaded as PDF or Excel. Push alerts arrive on the phone even when the app is closed.

3 · Manage the outlet (no billing)

Changes save to the BillerPe cloud and reach the outlet PC within about a minute while it is online. If the PC is offline, they apply when it reconnects, and the app shows "Waiting for outlet PC" until then. Printers stay on the outlet PC.

All names and numbers on these screens are sample data. The real app shows each owner's own outlets, synced from their outlet PCs. Full feature list and rollout plan: ROADMAP.md in this folder.
